# Target Market Research

## 1\. Industry

**FinTech AI**

The target market is companies operating at the intersection of
financial services and artificial intelligence. This includes companies
applying AI to financial problems such as fraud detection, financial
crime prevention, credit risk and underwriting, financial analysis,
document intelligence, automation, search and retrieval, and AI-assisted
decision support.

## 2\. Company Stage and Size

The target companies are **early-growth and growth-stage FinTech AI
companies with approximately 20--200 employees**.

This range focuses the campaign on companies likely to have real
technical and operational problems, an established product or customer
base, and some capacity to engage external technical support, while
remaining small enough for relevant decision makers to be accessible.

## 3\. Geography

The initial geographic focus is the **United Kingdom and United
States**.

The campaign will remain focused on these two markets rather than
targeting companies globally. This creates a bounded market for
personalised outreach while retaining a sufficiently large pool of
relevant companies to test the campaign.

## 4\. Decision Makers

**Primary decision maker: CTO**

The CTO is the primary target because this role is likely to understand
technical priorities, evaluate the relevance of AI engineering work, and
act on a potential scoped consulting engagement.

**Secondary decision makers: Founder and Head of AI**

Founders are particularly relevant in smaller companies where they
remain closely involved in product and technical decisions. Heads of AI
are relevant in companies with an established AI function and specific
implementation, evaluation, integration, or scaling needs.

## 5\. Relevant AI Problems

The campaign will prioritise companies working on problems aligned with
the FinTech AI landscape and the project directions developed during the
career-positioning research:

* Fraud detection and financial crime prevention
* Credit risk assessment and underwriting
* Financial analysis and research
* Financial document intelligence
* Automation of financial workflows
* Search and retrieval across financial information
* Anomaly detection
* AI-assisted decision support

## 6\. Trigger Conditions

A company becomes a higher-priority outreach target when one or more of
these signals are present:

* Active AI, machine-learning, or data hiring
* Recent funding
* A newly launched AI product or feature
* An announced data or automation initiative
* Rapid technical team growth
* A visible business or operational problem aligned with one of the
proposed FinTech AI projects

Trigger conditions provide a credible reason to contact a company at a
particular time. Recent funding alone will not be treated as sufficient
evidence of a relevant opportunity; stronger targets combine funding or
growth with a visible technical initiative, hiring need, product launch,
or project-aligned problem.

## 7\. Exclusion Criteria

Companies will be excluded from the initial campaign if they meet one or
more of the following conditions:

* They operate outside the FinTech or financial-services AI domain
* They fall substantially outside the target range of approximately
20--200 employees
* They are outside the United Kingdom and United States for the
initial campaign
* There is no identifiable AI, data, or automation activity relevant
to the campaign
* There is no visible business or operational problem aligned with the
selected capabilities or project directions
* No relevant technical or business decision maker can be identified
for personalised outreach

A company does not need to display every trigger condition to qualify.
Trigger conditions determine outreach priority and timing, while
exclusion criteria determine whether the company belongs in the campaign
at all.

## 8\. Why This ICP

This ICP focuses on early-growth and growth-stage FinTech AI companies
with approximately 20--200 employees, primarily in the United Kingdom
and United States. This market fits my chosen FinTech AI domain because
these companies work on problems such as fraud detection, financial
analysis, risk assessment, automation, search, and AI-assisted decision
support, which align with the AI problem areas identified in my domain
research.

The segment also fits the technical direction I am building toward. My
software engineering background gives me experience in building
production applications and integrating systems, while my current AI and
machine-learning learning is helping me develop the skills required to
build AI-powered solutions. My long-term interest and ongoing learning
in finance also give me a foundation for understanding financial
workflows and business problems rather than approaching them only from a
technical perspective.

The three proposed FinTech AI projects provide relevant evidence of my
ability to understand a domain problem, identify an appropriate AI
capability, work with relevant data, and build toward a measurable
business outcome. These projects can also support personalised outreach
by allowing me to approach companies with relevant work, observations,
or project evidence instead of sending generic messages.

Companies in the 20--200 employee range are narrow enough to create a
focused outreach campaign while still providing a sufficiently large
market to test. The ICP prioritises companies showing trigger conditions
such as active AI or ML hiring, recent funding, new AI product launches,
data or automation initiatives, rapid technical team growth, or visible
problems related to my project work. These signals provide a credible
reason to contact a company at a particular time.

The primary decision maker is the CTO, with Founders and Heads of AI as
secondary decision makers depending on the company's size and structure.
These roles are relevant because they are more likely to understand
technical problems, evaluate the relevance of my work, and act on a
potential consulting engagement or other collaboration opportunity.

The initial geographic focus on the United Kingdom and United States
keeps the campaign focused while providing access to a sufficiently
large pool of relevant FinTech AI companies. Overall, this ICP is
specific enough to support personalised outreach and consistent company
selection, while broad enough to test demand and refine the target
market based on campaign results.
